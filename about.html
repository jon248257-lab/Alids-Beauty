About ALIDS

Your Luxury Beauty Destination in Kigumba

Our Story

ALIDS Beauty & Cosmetics was born from one simple frustration: Finding 100% authentic beauty products in Kigumba was impossible.

We were tired of fake products damaging skin and wasting money. So we decided to change that. Today, ALIDS is Kigumba’s trusted source for original skincare, makeup, haircare and fragrances.

From our shop at Kigumba Main Market, we serve hundreds of clients who now glow with confidence. Because beauty should be authentic, affordable, and accessible.
